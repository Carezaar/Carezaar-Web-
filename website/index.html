How it works

From first answer to first hello.

  1. 01

    Say what you need

    Care type, services, schedule, distance and pay range. Four short steps, and you can change everything later.

  2. 02

    See who fits

    Matches show distance and pay up front. Open a profile to see care types, qualities, languages and shifts. Save the ones you like.

  3. 03

    Request, then accept

    Either side can send a match request. Nothing happens until the other person accepts, and either of you can end it later.

  4. 04

    Talk it through

    A private chat opens between you, with replies, read receipts and a notification when something new arrives.
